2. What the beta provides

3. Document generations

One document generation covers one successfully delivered tailored CV or one successfully delivered cover letter. Generating both uses two generations. A regeneration is a new document and uses another generation after you choose it. Editing, uploading, downloading and exporting do not use generations.

A generation may be reserved while work runs, but it is used only after successful delivery. Failed or cancelled generation, duplicate or replayed requests and identified no-charge fallbacks use no generation; the reservation is released. A correction appears as a separate ledger entry.

4. Packs, checkout and promotions

Paid packs are one-off purchases and do not renew automatically. The server-owned catalogue and Stripe checkout show the fixed GBP total and applicable tax treatment before payment. The success or cancel URL never grants generations; only durable server confirmation can do so.

A founding promotion is not guaranteed by catalogue wording. A bonus becomes guaranteed only if the server atomically reserves an exact amount for that checkout before the stated expiry.

5. Cancellation, refunds and statutory rights

Where UK consumer law gives you a cancellation right for digital content, checkout must ask for your express request for immediate supply and acknowledgement of any resulting loss of the 14-day cancellation right before supply begins. This does not affect rights for faulty, misdescribed or undelivered services.

Contact support to request a cancellation or refund. A completed refund, partial refund, chargeback or dispute can reverse the corresponding unused document generations and appears in the ledger. If generations from that purchase were already used, the account may require manual review. Generations have no cash value except through a refund required by law or agreed through the payment process.
