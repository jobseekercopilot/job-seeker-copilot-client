Privacy Policy

Effective 9 August 2026

Location and commute features

When you ask Job Seeker Copilot to find a UK location or estimate a commute, we may use Google Maps Platform through our server-side Google Maps gateway. This processing happens only for the location or commute action you request.

Depending on the action, we send Google only:

  • the location text you type;
  • a selected Google place identifier;
  • origin and destination coordinates;
  • travel mode and the route options needed to calculate an estimate.

We do not send Google your name, email address, CV, cover letter, employment history, application notes, generated documents, or other unrelated profile data.

What we retain

Google autocomplete suggestions and place details are kept only for the active, short-lived selection session. Route distance, duration and provider responses are response-only and are not stored in saved jobs, profiles, analytics or logs. We may store the selected Google place identifier. Canonical postcode, display-name and coordinate fields saved to your profile come from Postcodes.io or another recorded non-Google source, not from Google Maps content.

Google terms and privacy

Google processes these requests under the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can use the supported non-Google postcode path when Google Maps is disabled, and you can change or remove your saved location and commute preferences in your profile.
